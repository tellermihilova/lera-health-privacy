Lera Health Monitor

WHOOP Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

Lera Health Monitor is a personal, non-commercial health integration operated solely for the WHOOP account owner's own use. This policy explains how the integration handles data received from WHOOP after the account owner gives explicit OAuth consent.

Data accessed

The integration requests only the following WHOOP permissions:

  • read:recovery — recovery score, heart rate variability and resting heart rate;
  • read:cycles — physiological cycles, Strain and related heart-rate summaries;
  • read:workout — workouts, activity Strain and related measurements;
  • read:sleep — sleep records, performance and sleep-stage durations;
  • offline — a refresh token used to maintain authorized access without repeatedly asking the account owner to sign in.

The integration does not request permission to modify data in WHOOP.

Purpose

WHOOP data is used to provide the account owner with a private combined view of sleep, recovery, activity and training data, identify trends, and avoid double counting equivalent observations from multiple wearable sources.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and retrieved records are stored on a private health server controlled by the operator. Access is limited by authentication, network controls, and restrictive filesystem and database permissions. Tokens are not placed in client-side code or public logs.

Sharing and service providers

Personal data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated purposes. Selected data may be processed by infrastructure or AI service providers only when necessary to operate the private health assistant or answer a request initiated by the account owner. Those providers do not receive permission to use the data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained while the integration is authorized and for as long as it is needed for the account owner's personal health record. When deletion is requested, active copies and OAuth tokens are removed. Residual server-local backup copies expire under the configured retention schedule within 14 days.

Revoking access

The account owner may revoke the integration in WHOOP account settings at any time. Revocation stops future synchronization. The account owner may also request deletion by contacting the operator using the contact method below.

Contact

Privacy, access and deletion requests may be initiated through the operator's GitHub profile. Do not include health data, OAuth credentials or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

This policy applies only to Lera Health Monitor. WHOOP is a separate service governed by WHOOP's own terms and privacy policy.
